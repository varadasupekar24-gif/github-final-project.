# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid offensive language and personal attacks.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions.
