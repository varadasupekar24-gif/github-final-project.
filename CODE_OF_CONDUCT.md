# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, identity, or level of technical knowledge.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Showing empathy towards other contributors.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behaviour.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other community communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take the following actions depending on the severity of the violation:

1. Correction: A private or public warning.
2. Warning: A formal warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. Temporary Ban: Temporary restriction from project participation.
4. Permanent Ban: Permanent removal from project participation.
